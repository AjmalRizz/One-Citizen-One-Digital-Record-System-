National Digital Infrastructure

Architectural Principles of OCODR

Built according to ISO/IEC 27001 guidelines and Sri Lanka Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022.

Cryptographic Identity

Each citizen receives a deterministic, non-forgeable Citizen ID (LK-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) and cryptographic verification QR token.

Zero-Trust Partitioning

Strict statutory boundary isolation ensures hospitals cannot access police files, and schools cannot view confidential health data.

Immutable Audit Trail

Every record view, document generation, and official credential access is logged with timestamp, user role, and IP signature.

Public Trust Portal

Enables instant public identity validation via QR code or ID without disclosing private health, legal, or financial records.

Statutory Access Control

Strict Role-Based Data Partitions

Every department operates inside its lawful statutory enclave with 100% auditable logging.

Ministry of Health / Hospitals

Role: HOSPITAL

Authorized to inspect and record clinical diagnoses, hospital treatments, allergies, and vaccination registries.

Excludes police, tax, education & travel files

Schools & Universities

Role: SCHOOL / UNIVERSITY

Authorized to view and record school education, higher education degrees, and national GCE O/L & A/L exam scores.

Excludes medical, tax, police & passport files

Police & Immigration

Role: POLICE / IMMIGRATION

Authorized to inspect national passports, driving licence validity, border clearances, and travel demerit points.

Excludes clinical diagnoses & academic grades

Registrar General's Dept

Role: REGISTRAR

Vital civil registry: Register new births with instant cryptographic Citizen ID, parents linkage, and birth credential issue.

Issues birth, marriage & civil status records
